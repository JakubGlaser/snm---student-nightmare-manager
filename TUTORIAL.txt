==================================================
  SNM – STUDENT NIGHTMARE MANAGER
  Player Tutorial
==================================================


WHAT THE GAME IS ABOUT
----------------------
You are a teacher, and your one and only job is to keep your students paying
attention. There are nine students in the classroom, and each of them has a focus
meter ranging from 0% to 100%. The problem is that focus constantly drops –
students get bored, stare out the window, scroll their phones. When a student's
focus hits 0%, that student gives up and drops out of the game (a WASTED sticker
appears). The game ends the moment every student has burned out.

So your goal is to survive as long as possible, keep your students focused, and
rack up the highest score you can. It sounds simple, but the further you get, the
harder it becomes.


PLAYING THROUGH THE GAME
------------------------
The game is split into levels. Each level lasts 45 seconds – at the top you can
see a progress bar that gradually fills up. When the time runs out and you still
have at least one student alive, you advance to the next level.

The game ramps up with every level: student focus drops faster and your action
cooldowns get shorter. Level 1 is a breeze – you only lose if you do nothing at
all. Around level 6 you can no longer afford mistakes, and from level 8 onward it
isn't enough to just "not mess up" – you have to use the right mechanics at the
right time. From level 10 the difficulty plateaus (it stops getting worse), but
it's tuned to demand nearly flawless play. On top of that, every level you clear
past ten earns you a +1000 score bonus, so lasting as long as possible really
pays off.

Your score climbs steadily every second (and climbs faster in higher levels),
plus it's boosted by the level-10+ bonuses mentioned above. When the game ends,
your score is recorded on the leaderboard.


HOW FOCUS DROPS
---------------
Every active student's focus drains continuously. Slowly in the first level, much
faster in later ones. Your job is to keep topping it back up using your actions
(see below).

WATCH OUT for the "group effect": students are split into three groups (zones) –
left, center, and right – the same as in the Question minigame. When one student
in a group burns out, their classmates in the same zone start losing focus a
little faster. And if two in a group burn out, it gets even worse. A single
dropped student can drag down the whole zone – so it pays to put out fires early
and never let anyone fall to zero.


YOUR ACTIONS (BOTTOM BUTTONS)
-----------------------------
You have four main buttons. Three of them (Joke, Fun Fact, Question) restore
focus, but they go on cooldown after use (you have to wait a bit before using them
again). The cooldowns get shorter in higher levels so you can keep up.

* JOKE – Starts a short minigame with a line sliding across a bar. The moment the
  white line crosses the bright "sweet spot" zone, press SPACE. A hit = you add
  focus to every student. A miss = you take a little focus from everyone. In
  higher levels the line moves faster and the sweet spot is smaller, making it
  harder.

* FUN FACT – You need to pick the funniest, on success, raises focus for all students and,
  on failure, takes a little from everyone.

* QUESTION – You pick one of the three student groups (left / center / right). The
  chosen group immediately gets a focus bonus, and then a fast reaction game (QTE)
  begins: letters appear on the students, and you have to press them on your
  keyboard as fast as you can. As long as you hit the correct letters, the QTE
  keeps going. If you press the wrong key or run out of time, the round ends. On
  top of that, during the question all students' focus drain is temporarily paused.
  In higher levels you get less time per letter, so you have to react quicker.

* SPECIAL (Wheel of Luck) – See the next section.


SPECIAL ABILITIES (WHEEL OF LUCK)
---------------------------------
When things get rough, you can "help yourself" with the superpowers of the SNM
teachers. The SPECIAL button spins the Wheel of Luck – once per level. Press SPACE
to stop the wheel, and depending on where it lands, you get one of these abilities:

* Sadluck AI slop – Instantly sets EVERY student's focus to 120%. A great rescue
  when the situation is critical.

* Josef's tobacco – For the rest of the level, all your actions are 2x as effective
  (bonuses from the Joke, Fun Fact, and Question are all doubled).

* Cenek's endless speech – For the rest of the level, student focus stops dropping
  entirely, but time runs 4x faster (the level flies by).

* DJ's failed calculation – Risk and reward: it takes 20% focus from everyone right
  now, but at the start of the next level everyone starts at 120%.

* Vítek's Lecture Skip – Lecture cancelled! It instantly moves you to the next
  level.

The wheel is a one-time use per level, so think carefully about when to use it.


ITEMS (INVENTORY)
-----------------
At the start of every level you get one random item added to your inventory, until
it's full (max 5 items). When you hover an item with your mouse, its description
appears. Clicking uses the item – this consumes it and frees up a slot. Each item
is themed around a media theorist and has a different effect:

* Marshall McLuhan – Your next Joke or Fun Fact counts double.

* Lev Manovich – Throws away all your current items and draws you a fresh, full
  set of new ones.

* Manuel Castells – Gives a focus boost to every student at once.

* Bolter-Grusin – Repeats the effect of the last item you used.

* Friedrich Kittler – Instantly clears all your cooldowns (Joke, Fun Fact,
  Question, and the Wheel of Luck are all available again right away).

* Donna Haraway – For a few seconds, your actions hit harder (a temporary bonus to
  their effect).

* Matthew Fuller – Slowly refills focus for all students over several seconds.

* Luciano Floridi – Rescues your weakest student and pulls them back up to a safe
  level.

* Claude Shannon – For a few seconds, completely stops focus from dropping for all
  students.

* Vítek – Instantly skips you ahead to the next level.


MENU AND SETTINGS
-----------------
Clicking the meter in the classroom opens the menu, which pauses the game. From
there you can resume the game, start a new game, check the tutorial or credits, or
go back to the main menu. In the menu you can also toggle sound effects and music
on or off. While the menu is open, student focus does not drop.


A FEW TIPS TO FINISH
--------------------
* Don't let anyone fall to zero – one burned-out student speeds up the focus drop
  for their whole group.
* Keep an eye on your cooldowns and rotate your actions so you always have
  something ready.
* Save your strong items and the Wheel of Luck for crisis moments in higher levels.
* The Question is great not only for its bonus but also because focus doesn't drop
  during it.
* The goal is to last as long as possible – every level past ten is +1000 score.

Good luck, and may your students stay awake!
